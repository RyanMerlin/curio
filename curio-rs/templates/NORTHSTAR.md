# NORTHSTAR

## Name

Curio

## High-Level Description

Curio is a Git-native enterprise intelligence workspace that keeps a structured markdown wiki as the source of truth and publishes a readable Confluence mirror for broader audiences.

## Charter

Curio exists to turn raw source material into durable, navigable knowledge with visible provenance and clear editorial states.

The workspace should stay opinionated about structure:

- publish only material that is useful and defensible
- keep ambiguous or weak content in review
- use taxonomy and branch indexes to make navigation intentional
- treat Confluence as the human-facing mirror, not the editing surface
